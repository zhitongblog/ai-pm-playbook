# Community cases

[English](README.md) · [简体中文](README.zh.md)

Cases from people who built something with AI. A prototype counts. So does something that didn't work out, if you can say why.

## How to submit

**The easy way:** [open a case issue](https://github.com/zhitongblog/ai-pm-playbook/issues/new?template=case.yml). The form asks what you built, a link, which AI tools you used, how long it took, where you got stuck, and what you'd tell others. A maintainer turns it into a file here, credits you, and adds you to [CONTRIBUTORS.md](../../CONTRIBUTORS.md).

**The direct way:** open a pull request that adds one folder per case:

```
cases/community/<your-project-name>/README.md
```

Copy [`_template.md`](_template.md) (or [`_template.zh.md`](_template.zh.md)) as the starting point. Write in whichever language you're comfortable with; we add a short summary in the other one.

Cases from the WeChat group are written up here only with the author's consent, and always with their name.

Each month we pick one submitted case as the case of the month and write it up in full, on the site and in our WeChat Official Account.

## What makes a good case

- **Something real you can link to**: a site, an app store page, a repo, or at least screenshots or a short video.
- **Numbers you can back up**: days, commits, builds, releases, users. If you don't know a number, leave it out rather than estimate.
- **Where you got stuck, specifically.** "The App Store rejected it under 2.1(a) because the sandbox was missing an entitlement" helps the next person. "Publishing was hard" doesn't.
- **One or two decisions and why**: something you cut, something you insisted on, a rule you set for the AI.
- **What the AI couldn't see**, and how you found it: a real device, a packaged build, a real user.
- **Honest credits**: if others worked on it, or it's built on another project, say so.

No marketing copy, no invented testimonials. A short, specific case is better than a long, vague one.
