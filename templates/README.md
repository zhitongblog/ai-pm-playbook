# Templates

[English](README.md) · [简体中文](README.zh.md)

| Template | Use it when |
|---|---|
| [Dev brief: seven questions](dev-brief.md) | Starting anything. Fill it in, paste it into your AI coding tool |
| [Spec template](spec-template.md) | A release, a refactor, or work split across parallel agents. Extracted from a real project plan (SoloMD v4.6, public in the [SoloMD repo](https://github.com/zhitongblog/solomd)) |
| [Launch self-check](launch-checklist.md) | Before calling something done, deploying it, or submitting it to the App Store |

None of these are forms to fill in for their own sake. They're the shape your words need to take so the agent builds the right thing, and you can even ask the agent to fill them in from a conversation and only confirm the result.
