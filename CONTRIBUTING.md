# Contributing

[English](CONTRIBUTING.md) · [简体中文](CONTRIBUTING.zh.md)

Thanks for helping. This repo grows from real experience: things people built, prompts they used, places they got stuck.

## Ways to contribute

| You have | Do this |
|---|---|
| Something you built with AI | [Submit a case](https://github.com/zhitongblog/ai-pm-playbook/issues/new?template=case.yml), or open a PR adding `cases/community/<project>/README.md` from the [case template](cases/community/_template.md) |
| A prompt that worked on a real project | [Submit a prompt](https://github.com/zhitongblog/ai-pm-playbook/issues/new?template=prompt.yml) |
| A question | [Ask it](https://github.com/zhitongblog/ai-pm-playbook/issues/new?template=question.yml), or post in [Discussions](https://github.com/zhitongblog/ai-pm-playbook/discussions) (Q&A) |
| Something to show | Post in [Discussions → Show & Tell](https://github.com/zhitongblog/ai-pm-playbook/discussions) |
| A typo, a broken link, a clearer sentence | Open a pull request |
| A nomination or a score to argue for the Top 100 PM rankings | [Discussions → Rankings](https://github.com/zhitongblog/ai-pm-playbook/discussions) or [top-pm-rankings](https://github.com/zhitongblog/top-pm-rankings) |

## Writing rules

- **Real things only.** No invented users, numbers, quotes or results. If you don't know a number, leave it out.
- **Plain and specific.** "Rejected under guideline 2.1(a): the sandbox was missing an entitlement" beats "publishing was tricky". No marketing copy.
- **Credit honestly.** If others worked on it, or it's built on another project, say so.
- **Both languages, same structure.** English lives in `X.md`, Simplified Chinese in `X.zh.md`. If you can only write one, that's fine; say so in the PR and a maintainer will add the other. Community cases can stay in the language they were written in, with a short summary in the other.
- **Relative links** between files, so they work on GitHub and in clones.
- **Don't paste paid playbook chapters.** The repo carries the method, templates, prompts and case summaries. Full playbook chapters stay in the playbook.

## Pull requests

1. Keep one topic per PR.
2. Check that your links resolve.
3. Describe what you changed and why in the PR description.

## Be decent

This is a place for people who make things to look at each other's work. Be specific when you critique, generous when you help, and don't post ads or spam. Maintainers may remove content that isn't.
